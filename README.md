# **Alice in Borderland (2020)**

> *"The value of life changes depending on where you are."*  
> — Arisu

## **Overview**
Alice in Borderland is one of my favorite series. It's a Japanese sci-fi thriller that shows what happens when people suddenly get transported into a deserted Tokyo and are forced to play dangerous games to survive. I really liked the suspense and the way the story makes you think about life, choices, and trust.

---

## **Main Cast**

- Tao Tsuchiya as Usagi
- Nijiro Murakami as Chishiya
- Aya Asahina as Kuina

---

### **Genres**
`Sci-Fi` `Thriller` `Survival` `Drama`

---

## **Plot Summary**
The story starts with Arisu and his friends, who suddenly end up in a parallel version of Tokyo after a strange event. There are no normal people — just dangerous survival games. To stay alive, they need to win these games and earn visas. If the visa expires, a laser from the sky will kill them. It's intense, emotional, and really exciting.

---

## **Why I Like It**
- The games are very creative and full of suspense
- It shows how people change in extreme situations
- The characters grow a lot through the story
- It made me think about what I would do in their place
